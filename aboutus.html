Approach

Research first. Prototype next.

For new engineering opportunities, we first understand the application and requirements, evaluate feasibility, and then move toward prototyping and validation.

01 — Understand

Study the problem, operating environment, interfaces and performance requirements.

02 — Develop

Design, prototype and test the technical solution using appropriate components and engineering methods.

03 — Validate

Iterate against measurable requirements before considering production or commercial deployment.
