Precision Engineering Division

Precision rotary interconnect systems, developed from first principles.

An engineering R&D initiative within Crimson Owl Tech, working toward high-performance slip ring technology — combined power and data rotary interconnects — for defence, aerospace and heavy engineering applications.

Early-stage — currently in design & R&D phase, no product shipped yet
The problem

A gap in India's rotary interconnect supply chain

High-performance rotary joints that combine power, high-speed data and RF in a single rotating assembly — used in radar antenna drives, UAV gimbals and precision industrial systems — are almost entirely imported today. The barrier is not machining capability; it is the technology architecture, contact design and validated qualification history required to be trusted in mission-critical systems.

Import dependency

Nearly all high-performance rotary joints for radar, EW and aerospace systems are sourced from abroad.

An unserved tier

Basic industrial slip rings exist domestically; combined power + high-speed data + RF assemblies largely do not.

A qualification gap

What separates a trusted supplier from an unqualified one is validated test history — built over time, not overnight.

Our approach

A staged platform strategy

Rather than attempting a full-specification unit immediately, the plan validates the core mechanical and electrical contact platform first, then layers in harder channels once that base design is proven.

STAGE 1 — CURRENT FOCUS

Core Platform

Architecture definition and engineering design for a power + data hybrid rotary assembly.

STAGE 2

Extended Channels

Additional signal types layered onto a validated core design.

STAGE 3

High-Bandwidth Path

Optical data path for full-bandwidth, high-channel-count applications.

STAGE 4

Qualification

Environmental and life testing toward defence-grade certification.

We are currently at Stage 1: engineering design and documentation. Detailed technical specifications, fabrication drawings and test protocols are held privately and shared under NDA at the appropriate stage of a conversation.
