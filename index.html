R&D methodology

Understand → Design → Prototype → Validate

New opportunities start with understanding the actual application and measurable requirements rather than assuming a product specification.

Requirements

Prototype

Develop an engineering concept and build a proof-of-concept where technically feasible.

Validation

Test against defined requirements and iterate before considering production.
